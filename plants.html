Areca Palm

Also known as the butterfly palm, this plant features feathery, arching fronds and is excellent for purifying indoor air. It thrives in indirect light and prefers regular watering. Its feathery fronds create a tropical feel indoors.

Snake Plant

Renowned for its hardiness, the snake plant has upright, sword-like leaves with green and yellow variegation. It's known to improve indoor air quality and requires minimal maintenance. It thrives in low light and can tolerate infrequent watering.

Spider Plant

Featuring arching green and white striped leaves, the spider plant is easy to care for and produces 'babies' that can be propagated. It thrives in indirect light and tolerates occasional neglect. This resilient plant helps purify indoor air by removing toxins. Its cascading foliage makes it a great choice for hanging baskets. Spider plants adapt well to various environments and grow quickly. They are also non-toxic, making them safe for pets. With its elegant, trailing leaves, the spider plant adds a touch of freshness to any space.
